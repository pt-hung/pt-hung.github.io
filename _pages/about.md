I am a **Ph.D. Lecturer and Researcher** in the Department of Mechatronics, Faculty of Mechatronics, School of Mechanical and Automotive Engineering at **Hanoi University of Industry (HaUI)**, Vietnam.

My academic background and research focus on bridging theoretical control engineering with physical robotic systems. Before joining HaUI, I completed my doctoral research at the **Japan Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (JAIST)** in Ishikawa, Japan.

### Research Interests
* **Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs):** 6-DoF omnidirectional multi-rotor platforms, custom flight control architectures (PX4/AM32), and aerodynamic disturbance rejection.
* **Robotics & Mechatronics:** Bio-inspired robotics, soft robotics, wheel-legged mobile robots, and kinematic/dynamic modeling.
* **Intelligent & Adaptive Control:** Gain-scheduled LQR, $L_1$ adaptive control, and machine learning applications in autonomous systems.
